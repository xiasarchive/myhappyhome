This home began construction during the Welcome to my Homepage web residency at The Museum of Human Achievement!

myhappyhome is an interactive online experience in which the user explores the parallels of the uncertainty that the world holds through a lens of RNG. This is explored in a space that most consider safe and most predictable, the home. Inspired by my own personal experience and frustrations with ruminating on anxiety within the home, myhappyhome serves as a reflection and form of exposure to these situations that constantly run through my mind. In doing so, I trivialize these fears as I represent them and the “correct” choices as if I live in a world in which these things do happen. Like all happy homes, this one will continue construction and maintenance as time moves forward.
